Review:
Present Perfect = have/has + V3 → connects past to now. Has reflected, has not addressed, has never witnessed.
Past Perfect = had + V3 → before another past moment. Had left, had internalized.
Future Perfect = will have + V3 → completed before a future point. Will have completed.
